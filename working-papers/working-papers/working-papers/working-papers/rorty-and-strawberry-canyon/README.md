# Strawberry Canyon and Richard Rorty

## Core Relationship

Strawberry Canyon shares with Richard Rorty a rejection of fixed essences and universal interpretive foundations.

The framework assumes:

> signals do not possess fixed meanings.

Their meanings emerge contextually through:
- propagation state
- gamma topology
- volatility ecology
- participation density
- macro regime
- adaptive system behavior

This is deeply Rortyan in spirit.

---

## The Rortyan Move

Rorty rejected the idea that truth consists primarily of correspondence to eternal structures.

Instead:

> meaning emerges through vocabularies, practices, and contextual usefulness.

Strawberry Canyon applies a similar move to markets.

A Bollinger squeeze is not:
- inherently bullish
- inherently bearish
- inherently explosive

Its meaning depends on the propagation ecology surrounding it.

## The Core Inversion

Mechanistic technical analysis assumes:

    signal -> outcome

Strawberry Canyon assumes:

    regime -> contextual signal meaning

The regime is assumed first.
The signals derive meaning from it.

---

## Difference From Classical Mechanism

Mechanistic finance:
- equilibrium
- optimization
- fixed signal interpretation
- volatility as noise
- markets as machines

Strawberry Canyon:
- propagation
- adaptation
- contextual meaning
- volatility as regulatory behavior
- markets as living systems

---

## Relationship to Functional Contextualism

ACT and functional contextualism ask:

> What function does this event serve in this context?

Strawberry Canyon asks:

> How does this signal function inside the current propagation ecology?

This is contextualism operationalized in markets.

---

## Key Distinction From Pure Relativism

Strawberry Canyon remains empirical.

The framework does not dissolve markets into language alone.

It grounds:
- propagation
- gamma
- participation
- volatility
- liquidity
- and state transitions

in observable adaptive behavior.

---

## Final Principle

Markets are not equilibrium machines.

They are adaptive propagation systems.

Meaning emerges relationally through participation in the event itself.

The observation and the participation are inseparable.
