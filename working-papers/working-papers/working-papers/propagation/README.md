# Propagation

## Core Principle

Markets are not static pricing machines.

They are adaptive systems that transmit, absorb, stabilize, saturate, and reorganize movement through time.

The primary question is not:

    Is price bullish?

but:

    Can movement propagate through the system?

---

## In Service of the 3-Month Rail

Propagation is not free-floating.

It occurs in service of structural rails.

For Strawberry Canyon, the 3-month rail is the primary spatial context:

- the 3-month high is the upper rail
- the 3-month low is the lower rail
- the 50% retracement is the equilibrium basin

Acceptance above the 3-month high is not merely a breakout.

It is evidence that the system is forming a new rail.

Force without topology has no meaning.

- SessionForce = directional force
- ROC = force persistence
- Gamma = curvature
- Participation density = sponsorship
- ATRP_RATIO = volatility regime
- SUI = energy efficiency
- 3M rail = structural topology

The regime is assumed first.

The signals derive meaning from it.

---

## Pragmatist Frame

Pragmatism asks:

    What does this do?
    What function does it serve?
    What is it in service of?

A signal is not a fixed truth object.

It is a functional event inside a system.

A Bollinger squeeze, a gamma level, a volume spike, or a breakout only becomes meaningful inside its propagation ecology.

---

## Propagation States

### Active Propagation

Movement expands and reinforces itself.

- directional memory persists
- participation broadens
- volatility often expands
- pullbacks fail shallowly

### Downside Transmission

Negative propagation dominates.

- lower equilibrium accepted
- rebounds fail
- force compounds through the system

### Orderly Stabilization

The system absorbs stress.

- movement becomes efficient
- volatility contracts
- institutional accumulation possible
- equilibrium migrates slowly

### Exhaustion / Absorption

Movement loses transmissive power.

- directional memory fades
- participation collapses
- propagation decays
- reorganization becomes possible

---

## Contextual Meaning

No signal possesses universal meaning.

The same signal can mean different things depending on:

- propagation state
- rail position
- volatility topology
- gamma regime
- participation density
- macro context

Meaning emerges from function inside context.

---

## Final Principle

The edge does not come from prediction alone.

The edge comes from recognizing:

- when propagation is strengthening
- when propagation is weakening
- when movement is in service of the rail
- and when the system is transitioning between states

Markets do not simply move.

They regulate movement.
