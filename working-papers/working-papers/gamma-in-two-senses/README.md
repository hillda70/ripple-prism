# Gamma in Two Senses

Most discussions of gamma focus exclusively on dealer positioning:

* long gamma suppresses movement
* short gamma amplifies movement
* hedging creates feedback loops
* volatility becomes path dependent

But another meaning of gamma exists simultaneously.

Gamma is also curvature.

Not price itself, but:

* acceleration
* deceleration
* inflection
* propagation shape through time

Dealer gamma shapes market behavior mechanically.

Geometric gamma reveals the behavioral footprint of that structure.

The market is not merely moving.
It is changing the way movement propagates.

## Core Principle

Dealer gamma and geometric gamma are two expressions of the same adaptive process:

* one mechanical
* one phenomenological
